## 5. Notable Sub-Splits & Structural Findings

- **`DRIVE:PUSH` vs. `DRIVE:TRUTH`/`DRIVE:LEDGER`:** `PUSH`'s "never ask a clarifying question, under any circumstances except safety" rule directly conflicts with checkpoint 11 the moment the ambiguity sits inside something government-facing (an RFI-response claim, a licensing term, a pricing commitment) rather than routine internal drafting. Idea 1 itself already deliberately leaves things open ("No revenue model finalized yet — deliberately deferred") rather than guessing on a high-stakes item. Adopted scope: `PUSH` for low-stakes/reversible drafting only; never for anything that will reach an agency, an integrator's legal team, or a pricing page.
- **`STE:LOCK`'s banned-modal ladder** (no *should/might/could*) is exactly wrong for a cold-outreach email or a legal-nuance disclosure, where "we believe this could be a fit" or "coverage may not extend to X" is doing real, load-bearing work — flattening it to a bare `can/will/must` would manufacture false certainty in the one place this whole project has been most careful not to. Adopted scope: `STE:LOCK` for procedural/instructional text only (install steps, RFI response formatting); `STE:WARN`/`STE:CHECK` remain unconditional for severity/safety-disclosure lines specifically, where risk-first phrasing is exactly right.
- **Naming collision: `AUDIT:FORGE` vs. `FORGE:PROMPT/ROLES/AUDIT`.** These are two unrelated commands sharing a family name by coincidence — one is an audit-fix pass, the other is a prompt-engineering family. Neither prior audit flagged this explicitly. Standing rule adopted: always write both in full (`AUDIT:FORGE`, `FORGE:PROMPT`); a bare `FORGE:` invocation is treated as ambiguous and asked about rather than guessed.
- **`ISSUE:*` and `FORGE:*` (prompt-eng) are HOLD, not REJECT** — carried forward from the prior audit's own distinction. A HOLD means the design cleared Persona A but has no current object to act on (no bug reports, no production prompt surface); re-score B, not A, once that changes.
- **`DECIDE:*`/`VERIFY:*`/`SCOPE:*`/`LENS:*` (Group III) were audited from one-line summaries only** — the full mechanism text wasn't in the supplied corpus (`MASTER_CHAIN_REFERENCE.md` references them as already-defined without reproducing the definitions). This is flagged per the Honesty Floor rather than silently treated as equivalent-confidence to the fully-specified families.
