## 3. Integration Architecture — Four Forms

### 3a. Session Shorthand
Typed as literal tokens in future Themis Federal work turns to compactly invoke an already-agreed discipline — no re-explaining the mechanism each time. Applies to: `BUILD:*`, `RECAP:*`, `DIAL:*`, `SCOPE:*`, `LENS:*`, `EXTRACT:*`, `MORPH:*`, `SYNTH:*`, `STE:LOCK` (scoped), `DRIVE:PUSH` (scoped), `COUNCIL:*` (named-decision only).

### 3b. Phase-Gated Macros — Relabeled (see §5, Persona A finding)
Idea 1's own `Day 1 / Week 1 / Month 1 / Year 1` labels imply a calendar that isn't actually within a solo builder's control (Week 1 depends on an integrator choosing to answer a cold email). Gates are relabeled by **milestone**, with the original chronology kept as a loose reference, not a commitment:

| Milestone (replaces calendar label) | Fires |
|---|---|
| **Pre-Contact** (was Day 1) | `EXTRACT:GRAPH >> SCOPE:LOCK >> STRAT:MAP >> STRAT:PREMORTEM >> SPEC:LOCK` (custom chain, replaces stock `MACRO:GREENFIELD` — see prior file §8, finding 9) |
| **First-Contact** (was Week 1) | `MACRO:PUBLISH` on the outreach draft, with `STE:LOCK` explicitly *off* for that draft (see prior file §8, finding 7) |
| **Pilot** (was Month 1) | `TEST:CASES` against the pilot spec; `MACRO:CALLIT` if the integrator relationship needs a real go/no-go |
| **Steady-State** (was Year 1) | `DOC:SYNC` before any federal-audit-facing exposure; `STRAT:PREMORTEM` re-run on any Black-swan-row event, not just once |

### 3c. Document/Artifact Layer — Three Living Documents, Not Five
Persona A's overhead critique (§5) is resolved here by merging what was originally sketched as five files into three:

1. **`Themis_Federal_Build_Ledger.md`** — every `SPEC:LOCK`/`SPEC:DIFF` output plus the running `BUILD:DEBT` log. One file, two sections: "Specs" and "Debt."
2. **`Themis_Federal_Decision_and_Claims_Log.md`** — every `DECIDE:GATE`/`STRAT:HORIZON` real commitment, dated, plus every `VERIFY:AUDIT`/`MACRO:FACTCHECK` result on an externally-facing claim before it ships. Open/deferred items (e.g., "no revenue model finalized — deliberately deferred") are logged here explicitly, not left as an implicit absence (see §7, finding 1).
3. **`Themis_Federal_Integrator_Pipeline.md`** — the 3–5 candidate integrators, outreach status, feeds `MACRO:PRIORITIZE`/`MACRO:CALLIT`.

### 3d. Dormant/Reserved — Event-Triggered, Not Phase-Triggered
`ISSUE:*` (first real bug report), `FORGE:PROMPT/ROLES/AUDIT` (first production LLM-facing surface), `PEER:REVIEW/MERGE` (first commit from anyone but the solo builder — an event, not a scheduled milestone, per §5's fix), `MACRO:PROMPTLAB/QUICKPROMPT/SQUASH` (same triggers as their parent families). These cost nothing to hold — no maintenance, no session overhead — until the trigger fires.

### 3e. Confirmed Throws — Not Relitigated
The `PROTOCOL_STANDARD_LIBRARY.md` override block; strategy-catalog Level 4 in full; Manufactured Outrage and Vaporware Pre-emption from Level 3. Same verdict, same reasons, carried forward without re-opening.

## 4. Practical Operating Model — How Themis Federal Actually Runs

- **Three files, not a sprawling system.** The Build Ledger, the Decision-and-Claims Log, and the Integrator Pipeline are the entire persistent footprint. Everything else is session-level shorthand that leaves no artifact unless it produces one of these three.
- **One hard, narrow gate — not a vague aspiration.** No externally-facing artifact (outreach email, RFI one-pager, pitch deck, licensing term) is finalized without `MACRO:FACTCHECK` having run in that same session. Narrower and more enforceable than "run `MACRO:PUBLISH` on everything" — it attaches to a concrete event (finalization), not an ongoing vigilance that's easy to let slide under time pressure (Persona A's finding, §5).
- **"Which hat" rhythm, with its honest limit stated, not hidden.** Builder hat (`BUILD/SPEC/TEST/DOC`) → Strategist hat (`STRAT/DECIDE/LENS/SCOPE`) → Auditor hat (`AUDIT/VERIFY/STE:WARN/FACTCHECK`), worn sequentially in that order before anything external ships. **Irreducible limit, stated plainly:** switching hats in the same session is not the same as an independent second reviewer — it reduces but does not eliminate the risk of a solo builder rubber-stamping their own recent work. This is exactly why `PEER:*` stays dormant-but-ready rather than treated as unnecessary; it is the honest backstop for what hat-switching alone cannot fully provide.
- **No parallel competitive-monitoring mechanism invented.** Themis Ledger's own weekly competitive-monitoring cadence (Official Product Report §13) is extended to cover IronArch and Compliance Sheriff too, rather than building a second, duplicate practice — checkpoint 10 applied to process, not just code.
- **Cadence is milestone-based, not calendar-based**, per §3b — nothing here runs on a forced weekly/monthly clock except the inherited competitive-monitoring pass, which already exists and already runs on that cadence.

## 6. Persona C — Overseer Findings on Personas A and B

- **Against sycophancy, a positive finding:** across the 22 scored items in the prior file, Persona B scored *lower* than Persona A on three (`ISSUE`, `FORGE:prompt-eng`, `PEER`) — genuine pushback, not uniform inflation toward "everything is useful." This is evidence against pure `SYCOPHANCY`/`ANCHOR_BIAS`, not proof of its absence, and is reported as exactly that — a data point, not a clean bill of health.
- **Composition-risk conflation, flagged before it became a real gap:** passing 22 individual families does not by itself certify that the *assembled* operating model works — a system-integration question distinct from a component question. The fact that this round exists at all, specifically to stress-test the assembly rather than re-score the parts, is the correct response; had this file simply re-stated the prior file's PASS verdicts as "therefore the operating model is sound," that would have been `CONFIDENCE_INFLATION` without new evidence.
- **`REPETITION_AS_PROOF`, a real finding, not dismissed:** `Idea_1_Themis_Federal.md` was cited as the justifying source in a large majority of both files' rows. Repeated citation of one document across many rows is *not* independent corroboration of anything — it's one source read carefully many times. This doesn't invalidate the findings (Idea 1 is the correct, and in most cases only, available source for Themis-Federal-specific facts), but it means the confidence level attached to "matches Idea 1's own framing" should be read as *internally consistent with the one document that exists*, not as *externally corroborated* — no second independent source has been checked against most of these claims, because none currently exists for a pre-outreach idea.
- **`UNVERIFIED_SELF_CLAIM`, corrected here rather than repeated:** the prior file stated a 40×3=120-check sweep was "run," with 12 findings reported. That phrasing implies a precise, mechanically discrete count of executed checks that can't actually be independently verified from outside the process that produced it. The more honest framing, adopted in §7 below and going forward: a **systematic sweep across the same dimension space**, not a claim of exactly 120 discrete verified operations. The findings themselves are unchanged and stand on their own merits; only the rhetoric around how they were produced is corrected.

## 7. Round 2 Chronological Simulation — Operating-Model Stress Test

Same 40-cell dimension space (10 categories × 4 milestones) as the prior file, now aimed at the **assembled operating model** rather than individual commands, with Persona C's framing correction applied: this is a systematic sweep, not a claim of exactly 120 discrete verified checks.

1. **Pre-Contact, Financial:** the Decision-and-Claims Log's default "Blocking Questions: none" would silently drop "no revenue model finalized — deliberately deferred" if not logged explicitly. Fix already reflected in §3c: deferred items are logged as open, dated entries, not left as an implicit absence.
2. **First-Contact, Legal:** "confirm Section 508's WCAG 2.0 AA alignment is still current" needs a fresh, live check — `VERIFY:AUDIT` alone re-checks internal consistency, not external currency. The Claims-Verification half of the Decision-and-Claims Log requires a dated web-search-backed re-check here, not just an internal-consistency pass.
3. **Pilot, Operational:** confirms the event-based `PEER:*` trigger fix (§5) — a phase-based trigger would have missed a first hire landing mid-pilot rather than at a clean boundary.
4. **Steady-State, Platform/Channel:** a named federal-procurement-policy shift (Idea 1's own Edge-case row) has no re-scoping trigger in the original sketch. Fix: any Black-swan/Edge-case-row event triggers a fresh `STRAT:PREMORTEM >> SCOPE:CUT` pass on the whole channel, not a local patch — already reflected in §3b's Steady-State row.
5. **Cross-milestone, Competitive:** no file in the three-document set has a home for ongoing incumbent-monitoring. Resolved by inheriting Themis Ledger's existing weekly cadence rather than inventing a parallel one (§4) — checkpoint 10 held even at the process level.
6. **Cross-milestone, Team/Capacity:** the hat-switching rhythm cannot fully substitute for independent review. Stated as an honest, irreducible limit (§4) rather than implied solved — the same discipline this whole project's source corpus already applies to its own components (e.g., Kindred Demon's stated inability to rescue single-source poisoning).

No further findings surfaced beyond these six; the sweep is reported closed at that point rather than padded toward a round number, consistent with Persona C's §6 correction.
